Gate 5: one strong synthetic world

An 11-node graph cable adapts ten edge conductances from local voltage-difference energy. The rule never sees an eigenvector, Oja weight, alignment score, label, or held-out metric. Shuffled credit gets the exact same update-value multiset but on the wrong edges.

correct local credit0.9002held-out alignment
What it establishes

Local, alignment-blind structural change can improve statistical↔physical alignment in this constructed branched substrate.

What it does not

It does not show that biological dendrites use this plasticity law, and one success is not a general principle.

Gate 6: make it survive other worlds

The Gate-5 rule and hyperparameters are frozen. Twenty-four deterministic worlds vary tree topology, input ports, sensor locations and input covariance geometry. Failures are kept.

local beats frozen11 / 24
mean local − frozen+0.0005
mean local − shuffled−0.0031
Corrected metric

Alignment is the normalized projection into the span of the three slowest visible non-uniform physical modes. That makes the score invariant to arbitrary eigenvector rotations inside a degenerate eigenspace.

score = ||Uᵀ w|| / ||w||
The result refuses to flatter us

The unchanged local rule is approximately neutral versus frozen overall and slightly worse than shuffled overall. One world improves by about +0.344; another degrades by about −0.301.

Gate 5 happened. Gate 6 says it is context-dependent.

Absolute projection scores are mostly high because a three-dimensional subspace occupies most of the four-dimensional observed sensor space. The informative object is therefore the within-world delta, not the raw ≈0.93 mean score.

Where the real biology points next

Aizenbud et al. (2026) make morphology hard to reduce to a branch counter: dendritic area and bifurcation geometry are strong predictors of modeled single-neuron functional complexity, together with nonlinear NMDA integration. Leterrier (2018) reviews the AIS as a plastic determinant of excitability. Those papers motivate two separate next questions; they do not retroactively validate this graph rule.

Next: world anatomy

Why do a few Gate-6 worlds improve enormously while others collapse? Examine geometry, electrical distance, observability, spectral gaps, local-energy heterogeneity and load proxies before changing the learning law.

AIS / output boundary — future hypothesis

After the dendritic analysis, test whether a slow output-threshold/excitability variable driven by dendritic load can stabilize behavior across changing upstream morphology. Keep fixed-boundary controls and call it an AIS surrogate, not a detailed AIS model.
