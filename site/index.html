Claim ledger

What is actually being claimed?

The project becomes easier to understand once biology, algebra, synthetic experiments and speculation are kept in separate boxes.

prior work

Established / inherited

Oja-style learning can recover a leading covariance direction. Dendrites can support nonlinear subunits. The AIS initiates and shapes action potentials.

exact

Exact in this model

Static linear branches collapse to one affine filter. Square branch responses form a quadratic form. These are algebraic identities, not biological discoveries.

synthetic

Earned here

Local structural adaptation can strongly improve statistical↔physical alignment in one constructed cable, while the same frozen rule fails to generalize robustly across 24 worlds.

open hypothesis

Still unearned

Real dendritic morphology reorganizes toward input statistical modes, or dendrite and AIS co-adapt into a self-consistent input-to-spike circuit.

The hand-waving picture

What we are imagining

Recurring statistics leave preferred directions at the synapses. Branches behave like a physical filter bank. Local nonlinear convergence makes those branch states computationally useful. The soma presents an electrical load. A trigger region converts analog state into an event.

The serious ledger

What the repo has earned

Exact identities. Deterministic synthetic experiments. Matched controls. Frozen machine-readable receipts. A mechanism audit. A many-world robustness test that was allowed to weaken the hypothesis.

Not robustly. That narrows the claim.

Next falsification

Stop adding architecture. Explain the boundary.

The immediate scientific question is why the same local rule helps some worlds and hurts others. Spectral gaps, sensor observability, topology, port geometry, basis rotation and conductance heterogeneity are candidate explanatory variables. Only after that boundary is understood does the soma/AIS load-compensation hypothesis deserve center stage.
